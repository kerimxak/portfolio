Eczane

The one I'd want you to open first.

iOS · live backend

On-duty pharmacy finder

In Turkey, only a rotating set of pharmacies stay open overnight and on holidays. Finding the nearest one usually means digging through a slow government site at the worst possible moment.

Eczane puts every on-duty pharmacy on a map, sorted by distance, with one tap to navigate or call. A FastAPI service caches the official roster so the app stays fast and the source site only gets hit once.
