Turkey's pharmacies take turns staying open overnight, and finding the one on duty means checking a different site for every province. This finds it on a map.

The hard part was the data. I reverse-engineered a nationwide dataset from e-Devlet and got government GPS coordinates for 99.7% of pharmacies, so the map is accurate rather than approximate.

A halftone screen generator that thinks in millimetres. It turns a photograph into two-colour vector geometry you can 3D print, laser engrave or plot — six screen types, and a minimum feature size so nothing comes out smaller than your nozzle can make.

Dots merge through a signed-distance field rather than pairwise, which is what lets them flow together instead of looking glued.

Built with
Vanilla JS · Canvas · SVG

Output

Graduation project: a robot that rides a lift on its own. It finds the call button, presses it, waits, goes in, and picks the floor — which sounds small until you try it in a building it has never seen.

Vision runs on a Raspberry Pi, motor control on an ESP32 over BLE, and the two talk through ROS2.
